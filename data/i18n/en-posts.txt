178|T|‘NDOP: cloth of the royal courts and secret societies of Cameroon’, the new book by our president Ly Dumas
178|S|Ndop – you are at once ceremonial cloth, weaving tradition, a mythology of signs and a source of inspiration for today’s designers. Worn by the kings, queens and dignitaries of the chiefdoms of West Cameroon, you link the living and the dead, the invisible, the earth and the cosmos. ‘NDOP: cloth of the royal courts and secret societies of Cameroon’ by Ly Dumas is available at the Jean-Félicien Gacha Foundation, the Zingana hotel, FNAC Douala, and soon in museums in Cameroon and abroad. Each purchase helps fund our cultural and community work.
